# Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, productive, and respectful open-source environment for all contributors and users of Minima.

---

## Standards of Behavior

Positive behaviors include:
- Respectful and constructive technical discourse.
- Focusing on code quality, user privacy, and adherence to intentional design principles.
- Providing thoughtful feedback on Pull Requests and issue reports.
- Being receptive to architectural critique and design rationale.

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or hostile communication.
- Introducing disruptive, attention-capturing, or tracking code that violates Minima's anti-addiction philosophy.
- Pushing unvetted third-party dependencies or proprietary SDKs.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.
